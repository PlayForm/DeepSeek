# The Compound

The site is too thin because it has twenty-three shallow pages. The fix is fewer pages that each
carry five or six visual machines, and a plugin page that is a composition rather than a fact sheet.

## The rule

A page earns a nav entry only when it has its own subject. Two pages with the same subject are one
page with two sections. A component earns a place on a page only when it adds a dimension the prose
cannot: time, order, branch, size, before/after, or two axes at once. A second picture of one fact
is a defect.

Twenty-three entries become twelve, and the nine that fold into another page keep their content as a
section with its own heading, so a link to the old page still lands somewhere true.

## The plugin page

A plugin page is not a fact sheet with sections. It is a composition of five or six machines chosen
for that plugin's kind, in this order:

1. **The claim**: one sentence, and the question the page answers.
2. **The place**: where the plugin stands in the harness, drawn: the stage rail or the seam fan-out.
3. **The change**: the same conversation twice with one line marked, or the payload before and
   after.
4. **The dimension**: the one operator that plugin's kind calls for (matrix, timeline, ledger,
   ladder, fork, chain, diff).
5. **The mechanism**: what it reads and writes, as a schema and a pipeline, with the seam marked.
6. **The evidence**: the ledger line, the state it unlocks, and the fact the log must carry.

The eight harness plugins and the sixty family packages each get that composition, bespoke per
plugin: two plugins whose kinds differ do not share a section list, and two plugins whose kinds
agree may share the machines but not the words.

## The permutations

Every component's variants are exercised once, in the dev-only library, which is the page that
proves the vocabulary rather than a page a reader visits. A variant that no page uses is either a
variant the library should not have or a page that has not been written yet, and the library says
which.
