# The Audit

The audit fixes the accent budget, the weight ladder, and the state inventory before any edit, so
the pass has an acceptance list instead of a mood.

## The rule

Read the page and the stylesheet whole, then the token layer and the material mixins.

List every band with its geometry, its padding, its top rule, and its accent placements.

Count every accent placement and name its meaning in three words; write the five meanings the pass
keeps and the placements it moves to geometry.

List every control and the states it declares today, and every fact an interaction could reveal.

Write the table and the acceptance list into `Documentation/Design/Pass-Log.md` before the first
edit.

## The detail

The manual sheet's audit found eleven bands at `padding-block: var(--rhythm-head)`, all with a 1px
`--color-line-strong` top rule, one hover on the mode pills, and the accent on the diamonds, the
replace exits, the ledger panel, both pair plates, the chain's hops, the caption keys, the lamps,
the cards, and the modes at once.

The acceptance list was three sentences: no band may keep the same top-rule weight as its neighbor,
no accent may appear without a named meaning, and every control must have a focus state visible on
the thing the eye is on.

The work order is fixed: geometry first, states second, interactions third, motion last, because a
state added to a flat box is still a flat box.

The audit entry belongs in the log before the code. The design agent assembled the audit in
reasoning and wrote it into the log after the page existed, so its audit entry is a
reconstruction; write yours first.

The evidence is `Documentation/Design/Dissection-Design.md`, section 2, phase A, and
`Documentation/Design/Pass-Log.md`, Step 14.
