# THE WORKSPACE FAMILY - THE AUTHORITY SERVICE

> This is a granular document of the Workspace Plugin Family Decomposition. The parent hub is
> Documentation/Plans/WORKSPACE-PLUGIN-FAMILY-DECOMPOSITION.md. THE FRAMING (binding): the workspace
> family is the DEEPSEEK HARNESS'S OWN REFACTORING into the WORKSPACE-CENTRIC RUNTIME, powered by
> the @playform plugin family, and never a single plugin.

---

- **THE PACKAGE**: `@playform/plugin-dsh-workspace-authority`, the SERVICE, which takes the
  service-provider role per the factory precedent, where the shared services are the ones the others
  consume.
- **THE ROLE** (the family map): the capabilities, the leases, and the eviction rule, which give the
  grant, hold, and revoke behavior and the LICENSES.
- **THE LICENSE SEMANTICS**: the licenses are the concrete authority records of the
  Representation-First research's contract: the holder, the resource, the permitted operations, the
  expiration, the delegation rules, and the revocation. The scheduling priority and the graph weight
  live in SEPARATE fields, because the license is the authority and the weight is the
  representation, and the license and weight conflation is a logged pitfall.
- **THE REGIME**: the capabilities, leases, and evictions work in the workspace regime (the third
  axis) and authorize the workspace's INTERACTIONS, which is the scoped authorization. Authority is
  NOT knowledge, which is why the five judgments stay separate. The OS's own licensing in the
  physical and execution regimes stays the root licensor, because the representation never replaces
  reality. The typing home is INVERSE-REPRESENTATION.md, where the situated interaction protocols
  are authoritative.
- **THE ORCHESTRATION POSITION**: "the authority licenses" is the step after the govern gates in the
  invisible flow: ... -> the govern gates -> the authority licenses -> the approve binds -> ...
- **THE DISTRIBUTION LAW**: the package keeps its own Config, ledger strings, and tools, and the
  recursion prevention makes every event carry the operation identifier, the parent identifier, and
  the event category.
- **THE TWINS**: the Classic and ETS twins follow the family contract.
