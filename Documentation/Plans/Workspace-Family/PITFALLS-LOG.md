# THE WORKSPACE FAMILY - THE PITFALLS LOG

> Granular document of the Workspace Plugin Family Decomposition.
> The parent hub: Documentation/Plans/WORKSPACE-PLUGIN-FAMILY-DECOMPOSITION.md.
> From the day's work + the design's hazards.
> THE FRAMING (binding): the workspace family is the DEEPSEEK HARNESS'S OWN
> REFACTORING into the WORKSPACE-CENTRIC RUNTIME, powered by the @playform
> plugin family - never a single plugin.

---

- **THE EVENT-SPONSORSHIP GAP**: the fs/observed chain is
  filesystem-centric - the remote blocks' events need the event
  sponsorship's extension - the local-only assumptions in the governors'
  write-path + the ledger strings.
- **THE ADAPTER COVERAGE GAPS**: the binary files, the compound documents
  (the docx's OOXML - the zip-based XML trees!), the large files - the
  knowledge-graph extraction's cost + the freshness.
- **THE SEMANTIC UNCERTAINTY PER TYPE**: the rename means different
  things per format (the .ts symbol vs the .docx displayed text vs the
  .rs item) - the refusal-to-guess rule applies per adapter.
- **THE REPRESENTATION'S STALENESS**: the external edits without the
  events (the editors, the git operations, the remote syncs) - the
  qualified freshness + the digest-based reconciliation.
- **THE CLOUD/REMOTE RISKS**: the latency + the authentication + the
  version compatibility - the authenticated fallback rule.
- **THE INVERSE REPRESENTATION'S OWN FAILURE MODES**: the graph cycles,
  the dangling references, the evicted states mid-edit, the crash between
  the commit and the journal.
- **THE LICENSE/WEIGHT CONFLATION**: the priority, the retention, the
  permission never interchangeable - the license is the authority, the
  weight is the representation.
