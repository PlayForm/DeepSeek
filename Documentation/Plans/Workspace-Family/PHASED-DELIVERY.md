# THE WORKSPACE FAMILY - THE PHASED DELIVERY (THE ABLATION-ALIGNED ORDER)

> Detailed document of the Workspace Plugin Family Decomposition. The parent hub is
> Documentation/Plans/WORKSPACE-PLUGIN-FAMILY-DECOMPOSITION.md. THE FRAMING (binding): the workspace
> family is the DEEPSEEK HARNESS'S OWN REFACTORING into the WORKSPACE-CENTRIC RUNTIME, and the
> @playform plugin family powers it - it is never one plugin.

---

- **THE ORDER**: the ablation-aligned delivery, where each phase adds ONE capability layer so that
  the value of the family is testable at each step.
- **THE REGIME**: the whole delivery builds the WORKSPACE REGIME (the third axis), because the
  physical and execution regimes already exist and the phases add the OPERABILITY layer by layer.
- **PHASE 1 - THE ENGINE**: lib-dsh-workspace-live, the observe, the operate, and the merge (the v1
  engine: the schema, the state machine, the per-file commits, and the smokes with the fake clocks).
- **PHASE 2 - THE SAFETY**: the govern, the authority, and the journal (the authorization, the
  leases and evictions, and the recovery).
- **PHASE 3 - THE OPERATIONAL VIEW**: the operational view of the workspace agent (the graph-like
  projection and the attention projection through the context).
- **PHASE 4 - THE REFLECTIVE OPS**: the semantics, the evidence, and the approve (the reflective
  rename, connect, and replace; the validation objects; and the exact approvals).
- **PHASE 5 - THE MONITORS**: the monitor, the live, and the audit (the watch and sweep, the live
  view, and the accountability).
- **PHASE 6 - THE LATER STAGES**: the graph and the reflect (the weighted-graph carry and the
  code-block reflective API - the State:State -> State:Operational reference case).
