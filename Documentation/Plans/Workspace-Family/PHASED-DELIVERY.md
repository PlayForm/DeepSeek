# THE WORKSPACE FAMILY - THE PHASED DELIVERY (THE ABLATION-ALIGNED ORDER)

> Granular document of the Workspace Plugin Family Decomposition.
> The parent hub: Documentation/Plans/WORKSPACE-PLUGIN-FAMILY-DECOMPOSITION.md.
> THE FRAMING (binding): the workspace family is the DEEPSEEK HARNESS'S OWN
> REFACTORING into the WORKSPACE-CENTRIC RUNTIME, powered by the @playform
> plugin family - never a single plugin.

---

- **THE ORDER**: the ablation-aligned delivery - each phase adds ONE
  capability layer so the family's value is testable at every step:
- **THE REGIME**: the whole delivery builds the WORKSPACE REGIME (the
  third axis) - the physical + execution regimes already exist; the
  phases add the OPERABILITY layer by layer.
- **PHASE 1 - THE ENGINE**: lib-dsh-workspace-live + the observe + the
  operate + the merge (the v1 engine: the schema, the state machine, the
  per-file commits, the smokes with the fake clocks).
- **PHASE 2 - THE SAFETY**: the govern + the authority + the journal
  (the authorization, the leases/evictions, the recovery).
- **PHASE 3 - THE OPERATIONAL VIEW**: the workspace agent's operational
  view (the graph-like projection + the attention projection via the
  context).
- **PHASE 4 - THE REFLECTIVE OPS**: the semantics + the evidence + the
  approve (the reflective rename/connect/replace + the validation
  objects + the exact approvals).
- **PHASE 5 - THE MONITORS**: the monitor + the live + the audit (the
  watch/sweep + the live view + the accountability).
- **PHASE 6 - THE LATER STAGES**: the graph + the reflect (the
  weighted-graph carry + the code-block reflective API - the State:State
  -> State:Operational reference case).
