# THE WORKSPACE FAMILY - THE JOURNAL HOOK

> This is a granular document of the Workspace Plugin Family Decomposition. The parent hub is
> Documentation/Plans/WORKSPACE-PLUGIN-FAMILY-DECOMPOSITION.md. THE FRAMING (binding): the workspace
> family is the DEEPSEEK HARNESS'S OWN REFACTORING into the WORKSPACE-CENTRIC RUNTIME, powered by
> the @playform plugin family, and never a single plugin.

---

- **THE PACKAGE**: `@playform/hook-dsh-workspace-journal`, the EVENT HOOK.
- **THE ROLE** (the family map): the undo, the recovery checkpoints, and the crash recovery.
- **THE ORCHESTRATION POSITION**: "the journal records" is the step after the merge commits in the
  invisible flow: ... -> the merge commits (through Factory.Write) -> the journal records -> the
  audit lines -> the live view presents.
- **THE SAFETY PHASE**: the journal is part of phase 2 of the phased delivery, which holds the
  authorization, the leases and evictions, and the recovery, together with the govern and the
  authority.
- **THE REGIME**: the journal works in the workspace regime (the third axis) as the workspace's
  EVIDENCE record, which carries the undo, the recovery checkpoints, and the trace properties of
  recoveryRequired and recoveryIdentity. The OS execution axis stays orthogonal, and the typing home
  is INVERSE-REPRESENTATION.md, where the situated interaction protocols are authoritative.
- **THE CRASH SEMANTICS**: the recovery checkpoints and the crash recovery make the workspace's
  state survivable, and a crash between the commit and the journal is a logged failure mode of the
  inverse representation.
- **THE VISIBILITY RULE**: the journals are the INVISIBLE plugin flow, and the conversation never
  sees them until an event surfaces them.
- **THE TWINS**: the Classic and ETS twins follow the family contract.
