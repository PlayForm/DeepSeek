# THE WORKSPACE FAMILY - THE APPROVE HOOK

> This is a granular document of the Workspace Plugin Family Decomposition. The parent hub is
> Documentation/Plans/WORKSPACE-PLUGIN-FAMILY-DECOMPOSITION.md. THE FRAMING (binding): the workspace
> family is the DEEPSEEK HARNESS'S OWN REFACTORING into the WORKSPACE-CENTRIC RUNTIME, powered by
> the @playform plugin family, and never a single plugin.

---

- **THE PACKAGE**: `@playform/hook-dsh-workspace-approve`, the EVENT HOOK.
- **THE ROLE** (the family map): the exact human approval, which is the candidate-bound approval
  plus the constraints.
- **THE EXACT-APPROVAL SEMANTICS** (the Representation-First research): the approval binds to the
  EXACT candidate, so the human approves the concrete change set and never a fuzzy intent, and the
  constraints travel with the approval.
- **THE INTENT-MODEL SEMANTICS** (the critique round 5, binding): the approval is the Commitment,
  which records who accepted responsibility and is NOT the authorization, plus the
  AuthorizationDecision on the traceability graph between the ProposedChange and the
  ExecutionAttempt. AN INTENT MUST NOT AUTHORIZE ITS OWN MEANS, so the independent checks cover the
  contributes, the authorized scope, the policy, and the preconditions. The approval of an intention
  never authorizes the implementation the agent later produces.
- **THE REGIME**: the approve works in the workspace regime (the third axis) and binds the
  workspace's candidate to a human decision, while the OS execution axis stays orthogonal.
- **THE ORCHESTRATION POSITION**: "the approve binds" is the step after the authority licenses in
  the invisible flow: ... -> the authority licenses -> the approve binds -> the merge commits
  (through Factory.Write) -> ...
- **THE PHASED DELIVERY**: the approve is part of phase 4, with the semantics and the evidence (the
  reflective rename, connect, and replace, the validation objects, and the exact approvals).
- **THE TWINS**: the Classic and ETS twins follow the family contract.
