# THE WORKSPACE FAMILY - THE EVIDENCE SERVICE

> This is a granular document of the Workspace Plugin Family Decomposition. The parent hub is
> Documentation/Plans/WORKSPACE-PLUGIN-FAMILY-DECOMPOSITION.md. THE FRAMING (binding): the workspace
> family is the DEEPSEEK HARNESS'S OWN REFACTORING into the WORKSPACE-CENTRIC RUNTIME, powered by
> the @playform plugin family, and never a single plugin.

---

- **THE PACKAGE**: `@playform/plugin-dsh-workspace-evidence`, the SERVICE, which takes the
  service-provider role per the factory precedent, where the shared services are the ones the others
  consume.
- **THE ROLE** (the family map): the validation objects, the evidence dependencies, the four kinds
  of truth, and the stale-validation invalidation.
- **THE REGIME**: the evidence service works in the workspace regime (the third axis) and validates
  the workspace's claims, which are the qualified freshness and the OutcomeEvidence plus the
  justified assertions forall s in K(h) of the situated model. The OS execution axis stays
  orthogonal, and the typing home is INVERSE-REPRESENTATION.md, where the situated interaction
  protocols are authoritative.
- **THE FOUR KINDS OF TRUTH** (the Representation-First research): the evidence service owns the
  truth taxonomy, which decides what counts as proof for a representation, an observation, or a
  change.
- **THE EVIDENCE DEPENDENCIES**: the validation objects declare their dependencies, and the
  stale-validation invalidation fires when a dependency changes, following the
  incremental-computation principle and the Acar seam.
- **THE PHASED DELIVERY**: the evidence is part of phase 4, with the semantics and the approve (the
  reflective rename, connect, and replace, the validation objects, and the exact approvals).
- **THE DISTRIBUTION LAW**: the package keeps its own Config, ledger strings, and tools, and the
  recursion prevention makes every event carry the operation identifier, the parent identifier, and
  the event category.
- **THE TWINS**: the Classic and ETS twins follow the family contract.
