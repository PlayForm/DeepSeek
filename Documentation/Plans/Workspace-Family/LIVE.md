# THE WORKSPACE FAMILY - THE LIVE CLIENT

> This is a granular document of the Workspace Plugin Family Decomposition. The parent hub is
> Documentation/Plans/WORKSPACE-PLUGIN-FAMILY-DECOMPOSITION.md. THE FRAMING (binding): the workspace
> family is the DEEPSEEK HARNESS'S OWN REFACTORING into the WORKSPACE-CENTRIC RUNTIME, powered by
> the @playform plugin family, and never a single plugin.

---

- **THE PACKAGE**: `@playform/client-dsh-workspace-live`, the CLIENT half plus the framework, which
  takes the `client-dsh-*` role of the UI and CLIENT halves, the browser half.
- **THE ROLE** (the family map): the presentation, stable-until-event.
- **THE REGIME**: the live view works in the workspace regime (the third axis) and presents the
  workspace's REPRESENTATIONS, which are the presentations of the Entity, Presentation, and
  Interaction model. The OS execution axis stays orthogonal, because the presentation never
  conflates a running OS process with a managed entity, and the typing home is
  INVERSE-REPRESENTATION.md, where the situated interaction protocols are authoritative.
- **THE STABLE-UNTIL-EVENT CONTRACT**: the presentation does NOT re-render continuously, because it
  stays stable until an event surfaces a change. The live view presents at the END of the invisible
  flow: ... -> the audit lines -> the live view presents.
- **THE ORCHESTRATION POSITION**: the live view is the flow's LAST step, which is what the
  conversation sees through the workspace agent's surface.
- **THE VISIBILITY RULE**: the presentation IS the visible part, the operational view's rendering,
  and the machinery behind it stays invisible.
- **THE DISTRIBUTION LAW**: the package keeps its own Config, ledger strings, and tools, and the
  recursion prevention makes every event carry the operation identifier, the parent identifier, and
  the event category.
- **THE TWINS**: the Classic and ETS twins follow the family contract.
