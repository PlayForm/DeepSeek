# THE WORKSPACE FAMILY - THE LIVE CLIENT

> Granular document of the Workspace Plugin Family Decomposition. The parent hub:
> Documentation/Plans/WORKSPACE-PLUGIN-FAMILY-DECOMPOSITION.md. THE FRAMING (binding): the workspace
> family is the DEEPSEEK HARNESS'S OWN REFACTORING into the WORKSPACE-CENTRIC RUNTIME, powered by
> the @playform plugin family - never a single plugin.

---

- **THE PACKAGE**: `@playform/client-dsh-workspace-live` - the CLIENT half + the framework (the
  `client-dsh-*` role: the UI/CLIENT halves - the browser half).
- **THE ROLE** (the family map): the presentation, stable-until-event.
- **THE REGIME**: the workspace regime (the third axis) - the live view presents the workspace's
  REPRESENTATIONS (the presentations of the Entity/Presentation/Interaction model); the OS execution
  axis stays orthogonal (the presentation never conflates a running OS process with a managed
  entity). The typing home: INVERSE-REPRESENTATION.md (the situated interaction protocols
  authoritative).
- **THE STABLE-UNTIL-EVENT CONTRACT**: the presentation does NOT re-render continuously - it stays
  stable until an event surfaces a change (the live view presents at the END of the invisible flow:
  ... -> the audit lines -> the live view presents).
- **THE ORCHESTRATION POSITION**: the live view is the flow's LAST step - what the conversation sees
  through the workspace agent's surface.
- **THE VISIBILITY RULE**: the presentation IS the visible part - the operational view's rendering;
  the machinery behind it stays invisible.
- **THE DISTRIBUTION LAW**: its own Config + ledger strings + tools; the recursion prevention: every
  event carries the operation identifier, the parent identifier, the event category.
- **THE TWINS**: the Classic + ETS twins per the family contract.
