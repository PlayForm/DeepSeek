# THE WORKSPACE FAMILY - THE SEMANTICS SERVICE

> Detailed document of the Workspace Plugin Family Decomposition. The parent hub is
> Documentation/Plans/WORKSPACE-PLUGIN-FAMILY-DECOMPOSITION.md. THE FRAMING (binding): the workspace
> family is the DEEPSEEK HARNESS'S OWN REFACTORING into the WORKSPACE-CENTRIC RUNTIME, and the
> @playform plugin family powers it - it is never one plugin.

---

- **THE PACKAGE**: `@playform/plugin-dsh-workspace-semantics`, the SERVICE with the service-provider
  role and the factory precedent, which holds the shared services that the others consume.
- **THE ROLE** (the family map): the semantic model, which holds the symbols, the references, the
  deps, and the index.
- **THE CONSUMERS**: the OTHERS consume it, because the semantic layer feeds the operate proposals,
  the evidence validation, and the graph carry.
- **THE ORCHESTRATION POSITION**: "the semantics resolve", which is the second step of the invisible
  flow, right after the observe stamps.
- **THE VISIBILITY RULE**: the indexes are the INVISIBLE plugin flow, so the conversation never sees
  them until an event surfaces them.
- **THE REGIME**: the workspace regime (the third axis), where the semantic model is a
  Representation<Resource, Schema> (the knowledge graph = ONE representation) and the OS execution
  axis stays orthogonal.
- **THE DISTRIBUTION LAW**: its own Config, ledger strings, and tools, plus the recursion
  prevention, where every event carries the operation identifier, the parent identifier, and the
  event category.
- **THE TWINS**: the Classic and ETS twins per the family contract.
