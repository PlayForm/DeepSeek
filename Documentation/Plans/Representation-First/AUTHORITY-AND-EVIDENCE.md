# REPRESENTATION-FIRST WORKSPACE RESEARCH - THE AUTHORITY BENEATH THE PLUGINS AND THE OBSERVATIONS AS EVIDENCE (ROUND 4)

> A granular section of the representation-first workspace research, which covers the reconciliation
> mandate in round 4. The parent hub:
> Documentation/Plans/REPRESENTATION-FIRST-WORKSPACE-RESEARCH.md.

---

## 1. The authority beneath the plugins

- **THE ONE MANDATORY BOUNDARY**: ONE MANDATORY authorization boundary sits immediately before the
  external effects.
- **THE INTERSECTION RULE**: the effective permissions are the INTERSECTION of the applicable
  restrictions.
- **THE NARROWING DELEGATION**: the delegation NARROWS the authority and never enlarges it.
- **THE REVISION ATTACHMENT**: the policy and capability revisions attach to the approval records.
- **THE REVOCATION TIMING**: the revocation is handled BEFORE and AFTER the durable commit decision.
- **THE PROTECTION DISTINCTION**: the plugin-per-protection architecture stays, but the OPTIONAL
  EXTENSIONS are distinguished from the REQUIRED PROTECTIONS, so disabling a required protection
  disables the affected mutation and never quietly removes its restriction.

## 2. The observations as evidence (the qualified freshness)

- **THE ADOPTED FORMULATION**: the feature plan sometimes describes the fs/observed stamp as the
  attestation of reality, and the research qualifies the freshness by the observation mechanism.
  ADOPT the qualified formulation throughout, which holds the resource identity, the content digest,
  the observation source, the observed revision, and the known completeness limits.
- **THE TWO TESTS**: (a) an external edit occurs WITHOUT the corresponding harness event, and (b)
  the resource changes BETWEEN the final freshness check and the persistence.
- **THE HONEST LABELING**: the adapter documents its ACTUAL concurrency guarantee, because a fresh
  check, a cooperative lease, and the exclusion of the arbitrary external writers never share the
  same label.
