# Everything Is a Plugin: Inside DeepSeek Harness, the Week the Scaffolding Became the Product
Follow
16 min read Aug 24, 2026
Share
_A year ago, “the harness is the product” was an argument. On August 13, DeepSeek turned it into a strategy: an MIT-licensed agent harness where the models, the tools, the sandbox, and the loop itself are all swappable, and 90,000 GitHub stars arrived in two days. Here’s what it actually is, how it differs from the harnesses we’ve been building, and what the fine print says._
Image 01: monolith-vs-plugin.png — The load-bearing walls are now removable.
Some weeks the industry hands you your own thesis back with a bow on it.
On August 13, 2026, DeepSeek released a developer preview of something called **DeepSeek Harness** (`dsh` on the command line), alongside its V4-Pro model. Not a model. Not a chatbot. A harness: the loop, the tools, the permissions, the session log, the scaffolding that turns a model into an agent. The repository crossed 90,000 GitHub stars in about two days, which is the kind of number that usually needs a model attached to it. This one shipped with a philosophy instead, stated in five words in the README: **everything is a plugin.**
I wrote at the start of this series that the model is the engine and the harness is the car, and that the car is yours to build. DeepSeek’s bet is more aggressive: the car should be a kit. Models, tools, skills, sessions, sandboxes, filesystems, the UI, and — this is the part…
